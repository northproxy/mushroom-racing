# 10 — Glossary

## Aspect
Direction a slope faces: N, NE, E, etc.

## BFW
Bundesforschungs- und Ausbildungszentrum für Wald, Naturgefahren und Landschaft.

## Confidence
Estimate of how reliable/complete the input data are.

## Current Conditions Score
How favourable current weather and moisture conditions are.

## Flush / Schub
A wave of mushroom fruiting.

## Habitat Score
Long-term ecological suitability of a location.

## Hard exclusion
A rule that makes a location ineligible rather than merely lowering its score.

## Mycorrhiza
Symbiotic association between fungus and plant roots.

## Opportunity Score
Current combined recommendation score for a location.

## Podsol
Typically acidic, strongly leached soil common in some cool forest environments.

## Steinpilz
German common name used for several closely related bolete species; the initial model focuses on *Boletus edulis*.
